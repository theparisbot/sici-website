What Sici does

It works for you on day one, even if nobody else at your location has it yet.

1

Your record card, in plain words

Import a screenshot or PDF of your record card. Sici counts every window for you, shows how close you are to each limit, and counts down the days until you're back in the clear. Paris explains it without the jargon.

2

Every trade on the record

Log each trade and giveaway, including the ones you worked out in a group chat or face to face. When you wonder whether you gave that day away or picked it up, the answer is one tap away.

3

The Wire

Put a shift up for trade or giveaway, or say you're available to work. It goes only to coworkers at your location in your classification. A listing is a request, not an approved change.

4

The rest of your workday

Paris reads you today's schedule. Flight Plan times your commute and warns you about severe weather before a shift. The Union page puts your union a tap away. All of it in six languages.

Meet Paris

Your AI companion, right in the app.

Paris is Sici's AI companion: a small but mighty Jack Russell with big curiosity and an even bigger heart. She reads your record with you, tells you in plain words where you stand, and helps you think it through before you call in.

  • Ask Paris. Tap Ask Paris from any screen and ask in your own words.
  • No jargon. She explains your windows and points the way you'd explain them to a friend.
  • Your contract, cited. Ask about your contract and get a plain answer with the section it comes from.
  • Your day. She greets you on Mission home with what's ahead.

Paris can get things wrong, so check anything important against your record card.

Effective date: [date]. This policy explains what Sici ("Should I Call In") collects, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it. Sici is operated by [legal company name] ("we").

The short version

  • Your attendance record is private to you. We never show it to coworkers, crews, stewards or any employer.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • Sici reads your location only when you tap to use it, never in the background.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and sign-in details. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and are never visible to us.
  • Profile: your name, your work location and sub-location, your hire date, your language, and an optional photo.
  • Attendance records: call-outs and other entries you add, and record card screenshots or PDFs you choose to import.
  • Schedule: shifts you enter or import from a schedule screenshot.
  • Trades and posts: trades and giveaways you log or post, offers and claims, and messages you send in Comms.
  • Location: a home base point, only when you tap to set it, and your position at each checkpoint of a Flight Plan trip you start. No background tracking and no location history.
  • Calendar: only if you choose a calendar for Sici to add your confirmed shifts to. Sici writes to that calendar and doesn't read your other events. [confirm with the app]
  • Notifications: a device token so we can send the alerts you turn on.
  • Support: anything you send us when you ask for help or report a problem.

How we use it

We use your information to run Sici's features: working out your attendance status, showing your schedule and trade log, running the Wire at your location, timing your commute, sending the alerts you ask for, and answering your support requests. We also use it to keep Sici secure and to fix problems.

Who can see what

  • Only you: your attendance record, your record card imports, your status, your home base and your trips.
  • People in your location's group: your name, role, hire date, photo if you add one, and anything you post to the Wire.
  • People you message: the messages you send them.

Service providers

We use a small number of companies to run Sici. They process data only to provide their service to us.

  • Hosting, database and sign-in: Supabase, confirm
  • Reading screenshots and record cards: an AI text-reading service, OpenAI, confirm. The image is sent only to read its text, and you review the result before it's saved. [confirm the provider's data retention terms]
  • Push notifications: Expo, confirm
  • Weather alerts: the U.S. National Weather Service, using your work location's area. Your personal location isn't sent.

We may disclose information if the law requires it. We'll tell you when we can.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is open. You can delete your account in the app from your Account page. Deleting removes your sign-in and your account data. Records you shared with others, such as a trade another person was part of, may remain in their history, and copies saved on your own devices stay until you remove them. Common questions

How do I import my record card?

Take a screenshot or save a PDF of your record card, then import it in Sici. You'll see everything Sici read and can fix anything before it's saved.

Is my status official?

No. It's an estimate from what you entered. Your employer's records and your union agreement decide what counts. If something looks wrong, check your entries first, then ask your steward.

Who can see my attendance record?

Only you. It isn't shown to coworkers, crews, stewards or any employer.

Does a trade in Sici make it official?

No. Sici helps you find someone and keeps a receipt. You still complete the trade through your employer's own process.

My location isn't open yet.

Locations open one at a time, once enough people there ask. Everything that works on your own, like your record card and your trade log, works today.

How do I delete my account?

Open your Account page in the app and choose Delete account. You'll type DELETE to confirm. Your sign-in and account data are removed. Shared records, like a trade another person was part of, may stay in their history.

Which languages does Sici speak?

English, Spanish, Haitian Creole, French, Arabic and Portuguese.

Report a problem

Email [support email] with "Problem" in the subject line. For anything about someone misusing the Wire, include their name and the post, and we'll look into it.
